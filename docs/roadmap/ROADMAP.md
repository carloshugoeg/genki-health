# Roadmap

The course brief defines two deliveries. Only Entrega I is ticketed as current work.

| Delivery | What the brief asks | Queue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrega I | ER and relational models for the relational institutions. Entities, attributes, classification, normalization, and plain-language operations for all four. | `poteto-mode/queue/backlog.md` |
| Entrega II | Client-server app, four engines, corrected model, equivalent queries, document embed-vs-reference, engine-agnostic UI, archive flow, routing by institution, comparative note. | One future ticket, blocked by Entrega I. Not broken into implementation tasks. |

The 2026-08-27 architecture spec splits Entrega II into finer phases. Those phases are not tickets. Turn them into tickets only after Entrega I is accepted and a human asks for that split.

Deadlines written on the architecture spec were 8 September 2026 for Entrega I and 9 November 2026 for Entrega II. The Entrega I PDF in this repo is dated 7 September 2026.
